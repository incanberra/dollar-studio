THE DETAILS BEHIND THE DOLLARS

Clear sources. Consistent comparisons.

01 / THE CALCULATION

A basket of goods, in another year

Converted amount = original amount × destination price index ÷ starting price index. Results describe the cost of a comparable basket of consumer goods and services.

CPI measures general consumer prices. Wages, house prices, individual products and investment returns can move differently.

02 / THE HISTORY

From 1850 to the present

1850–1900: Sydney retail price estimates, a local historical proxy. 1901–1948: linked Australian retail price indexes. 1949–2025: averages of the four quarterly all-groups CPI observations.

Early indexes cover fewer goods and are rounded. Long comparisons are approximate. The old series is linked to the modern CPI using their complete 1949 annual averages.

Understanding money before 1966

Australia introduced dollars on 14 February 1966 at a rate of two dollars to one Australian pound. Earlier dollar amounts in this app are hypothetical dollar equivalents. For an actual historical price in pounds, select “Australian pounds” above. The app converts it to dollar units before adjusting for price changes.

For years before 1901, the underlying prices refer to Sydney in colonial Australia. These results are approximate comparisons, not a national CPI measurement.
